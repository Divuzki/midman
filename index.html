How It Works

Five simple steps to secure your transaction.

1

Create an Agreement

One party sets the terms — outcome, deposit amount, and deadline. Middleman acts as a neutral escrow, not a participant.

2

Secure Funding

Both parties fund the escrow transaction. Assets are locked securely and cannot be accessed by either party until terms are met.

3

Transaction Is Confirmed

When the event ends, the agreed outcome is submitted. If both parties agree, the process continues instantly.

4

Dispute Resolution

If a dispute occurs, our support team reviews the transaction details and resolves the case according to the agreement terms.

5

Funds Released

Funds are released to the approved party. Middleman only charges a transparent service fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Middleman is an escrow platform that helps buyers and sellers transact with greater confidence through agreed terms, a structured payment process, and delivery confirmation before funds are released.
Create an agreement, invite the other party, and have both parties accept the terms. The buyer funds the agreement, the seller delivers the goods or services, and the buyer confirms receipt before funds are released according to the agreement and applicable procedures.
Middleman provides a structured process for handling transaction funds and resolving disagreements. However, no platform can guarantee that every transaction is risk-free. Always review the agreement terms and report suspicious activity promptly.
You can raise a dispute through Middleman. Ordinary fund release is stopped while the dispute is handled, and the agreement and available evidence are reviewed before an outcome is determined.
After the buyer confirms receipt, funds are released according to the agreement and applicable procedures. Released funds go to the recipient's Middleman wallet first, after which they can withdraw eligible funds to a matching bank account.
You can create and complete agreements without identity verification. However, KYC approval is required before withdrawing funds, and your withdrawal bank account must match your verified identity.
Contact Middleman support with your transaction reference and relevant details. If a payment is delayed, do not repeat it simply because it has not appeared yet.
Use the support options available on the Middleman website or within your account. Middleman's support acknowledgment target is within one hour, although resolution times depend on the issue.
